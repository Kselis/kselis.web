Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

Last Updated: July 01, 2026

1. Our Commitment

KSELIS is deeply committed to fostering a safe, respectful, and inclusive learning environment for every member of our community. We believe that everyone deserves the right to learn, collaborate, mentor, and contribute without fear of harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.

Sexual harassment of any form is strictly prohibited across all KSELIS platforms, activities, and events.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all individuals participating in or representing KSELIS, including but not limited to:

  • Students and Learners
  • Volunteers and Mentors
  • Community Maintainers and Moderators
  • Interns and Contributors
  • Visitors participating in official KSELIS spaces

The policy applies to interactions occurring through all official mediums, including:

  • The official KSELIS Website
  • Discord Community and Voice Channels
  • Email Communications
  • Video Conferences and Online Workshops
  • Community Events
  • Social Media managed by KSELIS
  • Offline events organized by KSELIS

3. What Constitutes Sexual Harassment

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behavior of a sexual nature that creates an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment.

Examples include, but are not limited to:

  • Unwelcome sexual advances or requests for sexual favors.
  • Sexually suggestive remarks, jokes, or gestures.
  • Repeated unwanted flirting or inappropriate comments regarding someone's appearance or body.
  • Sending sexually explicit messages, images, or sharing pornography/obscene material.
  • Persistent unwanted direct messages, stalking, or intimidation.
  • Physical contact without explicit consent.
  • Threatening or coercive behavior related to sexual conduct.
  • Retaliating against someone for rejecting advances or reporting misconduct.

Harassment may occur regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or position within the community.

4. Online Conduct

As KSELIS primarily operates through digital platforms, members are expected to maintain professional and respectful behavior during all online interactions. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Sending inappropriate direct messages or harassing users in Discord channels.
  • Sharing explicit media of any kind.
  • Recording meetings without explicit permission from all participants.
  • Using voice channels to intimidate or harass others.
  • Repeated unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
  • Creating alternate accounts to bypass blocks or continue harassment.
  • Misusing moderator or administrative privileges.

Respecting personal boundaries is mandatory at all times.

5. Reporting an Incident

Anyone who experiences or witnesses sexual harassment is strongly encouraged to report the incident promptly. To help us take effective action, reports should include, where possible:

  • The name or username of the individual involved.
  • The date and approximate time of the incident.
  • The platform where the incident occurred.
  • A detailed description of the incident.
  • Screenshots or supporting evidence (if available).

Reports may be submitted directly through our official contact channels listed at the bottom of this policy.

6. Confidentiality

KSELIS treats all complaints with the utmost confidentiality. Information related to a complaint will only be shared with individuals directly involved in reviewing or resolving the matter, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Review Process

Upon receiving a complaint, the KSELIS core team will:

  1. Acknowledge receipt of the complaint promptly.
  2. Review all available information and evidence.
  3. Contact relevant parties privately if additional information is required.
  4. Evaluate the complaint fairly and impartially.
  5. Determine appropriate corrective measures based on the evidence.

Every effort will be made to handle complaints promptly, professionally, and respectfully.

8. Possible Actions

Depending on the severity of the misconduct, KSELIS may take one or more of the following actions:

  • Educational or formal warnings.
  • Immediate removal of messages, posts, or content.
  • Temporary communication restrictions (e.g., server mutes).
  • Suspension from KSELIS platforms.
  • Permanent removal and banning from the community.
  • Removal from volunteer, moderator, or leadership positions.
  • Restriction from attending future events.
  • Referral to appropriate authorities where required by law.

9. Protection Against Retaliation

KSELIS strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports misconduct in good faith or participates in an investigation. Acts of retaliation will not be tolerated and may themselves result in immediate disciplinary action.

10. False or Malicious Complaints

Knowingly making false or malicious allegations undermines community trust and the integrity of this policy. Individuals found to have intentionally submitted fabricated complaints may be subject to appropriate disciplinary action. This provision does not apply to complaints made in good faith that cannot ultimately be substantiated.

11. Responsibilities of Community Members

Every member of KSELIS is expected to:

  • Treat others with dignity and respect.
  • Maintain professional communication.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Report inappropriate behavior.
  • Cooperate during investigations.
  • Help create an inclusive, welcoming learning environment.

12. Leadership Responsibilities

KSELIS leadership, moderators, and community maintainers hold a higher standard and are expected to:

  • Lead by example and set the standard for community conduct.
  • Take all reports seriously.
  • Respond fairly, impartially, and without bias.
  • Maintain strict confidentiality.
  • Take timely and appropriate corrective action.

13. Compliance

KSELIS currently operates as an independent educational initiative. As the organization evolves, this policy may be updated to align with applicable laws and regulatory requirements, including the Prevention of Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, where applicable.

14. Changes to This Policy

KSELIS reserves the right to modify or update this policy at any time. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.

15. Contact

Email: kselis.institute@gmail.com

By participating in KSELIS communities, platforms, or events, you agree to uphold the principles outlined in this policy and contribute to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment for everyone.
